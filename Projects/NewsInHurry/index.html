News for People in a Hurry helps you catch up fast. It brings in live top stories from the New York Times (business, tech, world, politics, and more) and shows them as simple cards you can scan in a few minutes. You choose how far back to look (6 to 48 hours) and can filter by mood if you want; the app adds short AI summaries and highlights what is breaking or trending first. When you want the bigger picture, Signal Studio looks across all sections, explains how stories connect, sums up the overall mood, and checks that picture against live market data. You can also open a longer research note on any headline when you need more depth.

Design principle: show useful content quickly, then upgrade intelligence progressively in the background.

The problem

Why another news surface?

Busy readers need signal, not noise. Raw feeds across many sections are hard to scan; seeing how stories connect and whether markets align takes more time than most people have.

The app packages live NYT Top Stories, AI-ranked cards (breaking, trending, latest), and a multi-agent analysis layer so users get scannable headlines first and deeper synthesis when they want it.

What readers get

From headline to validated insight

  • NYT Top Stories across home, business, arts, technology, world, and politics.
  • Breaking → trending → latest ordering with a 6 to 48 hour window and optional sentiment filter.
  • AI sentiment, impact labels, and tone-aware summaries (informational, opinion, analytical).
  • Six cards per page with pagination; first page uses a 2+2+2 mix.
  • Global Insight marquee and Signal Studio with QC metrics and PDF report.
  • Dive deeper per article via a separate research agent (Wikipedia + Yahoo Finance tools).

Signal Studio

Three agents, one orchestrated workflow

Agent 1 Cross-section themes, triggers, and propagation across desks.
Agent 2 World mood score, market stance, and reasoning from linked stories.
Agent 3 Validates the news narrative against live market data, with extra market lookups when needed.

Parallel section briefs run first; agents 1 → 2 → 3 run in series toward the marquee and Signal Studio dashboard. See the full documentation.

How the pipeline runs

From Refresh News to live intelligence

flowchart TD
  refresh[Refresh News] --> nyt[Fetch NYT sections]
  nyt --> feed[Publish card feed]
  feed --> enrich[Sentiment impact summaries]
  enrich --> packets[Section packets]
  packets --> briefs[Parallel briefs]
  briefs --> agents[Agents 1 2 3]
  agents --> ui[Marquee + Signal Studio]
PhaseWhat happens
Quick feedCards appear fast; placeholders until scoped AI enrichment finishes.
EnrichmentSentiment, impact, and tone-aware summaries cached per article.
AgentsFallback studio first; full LLM pass upgrades marquee and tabs.
ResearchOptional per-article deep brief on demand.

Under the hood

Shiny for Python · reactive state · no database

  • UI: tabs, filters, cards, Signal Studio, marquee.
  • Data: NYT fetch, dedupe, breaking/trending/latest ranking.
  • AI: sentiment, impact, summaries, agent JSON outputs.
  • Agents: workflow.py, llm_client.py, market snapshot via yfinance.

Deployed on Posit Connect (AppV1/Procfile).
